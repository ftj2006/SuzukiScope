SuzukiScope connects to an ELM327 OBD-II adapter (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) to read live diagnostic data from your Suzuki vehicle's CAN bus.

Features:
- Configurable live gauges (oil/water temperature, boost pressure, RPM, throttle, speed, and more)
- Warning/critical thresholds with colour-coded gauges and alerts
- Session logging with CSV export
- Diagnostic trouble code (DTC) reading
- Android Auto support with a dashboard view for use while driving

Requires a compatible ELM327 Wi-Fi or Bluetooth adapter plugged into your vehicle's OBD-II port.
